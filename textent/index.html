Turn complex, changing documents into decisions you can defend.

01THE PRODUCT

A decision system for documents that keep changing.

Search can find a passage and chat can answer a question. Textent is designed to preserve the review: what changed, why it matters, which source proves it, what action follows, and how the reviewer decided.

REUSE Start from a trusted version.

Protect the original, generate the working copy, and surface what must change before reuse.

COMPARE Find the changes that alter meaning.

Separate material revisions from formatting noise and open both source passages together.

DECIDE Turn evidence into accountable action.

Expose assumptions and missing evidence, then accept, assign, revise, or defer with a recorded reason.

THE DIFFERENCE

Not another “chat with documents” interface. The design keeps document lineage, exact evidence, bounded AI judgment, human approval, and the audit trail together from intake to disposition.

01Event-driven AWS scale

Direct uploads, queue backpressure, retries, DLQs, and independently scalable workers keep heavy document work off the request path.

02Bounded AI judgment

A benchmark-selected model may classify, rank, or explain candidates; evidence checks and human approval control the outcome.

03Cost and governance by design

Admission limits, concurrency, token ceilings, encryption, content-free telemetry, retention, deletion, alarms, and a kill switch shape the workflow.

04THE EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Credible because the claims are separated.

The public case study distinguishes verified implementation evidence from target architecture and conditional enterprise direction.

VERIFIED OFFLINE Document and trust contracts

DOCX structure checks, main-body extraction, source-bound evidence anchors, admission limits, fail-closed evidence checks, workflow contracts, and automated verification.

DESIGNED · NOT DEPLOYED AWS operating architecture

Asynchronous intake, processing boundaries, bounded Bedrock use, metadata state, identity, encryption, observability, cost controls, deletion, and recovery.

CONDITIONAL · NOT IMPLEMENTED Enterprise retrieval and workflow

Permission-aware connectors, hybrid retrieval, evidence-backed RAG, controlled generation, document-native revisions, approvals, and durable orchestration where justified.
