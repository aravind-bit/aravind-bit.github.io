03THE EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Built to be inspected—without being mistaken for production.

The public case study separates what is verified, what is designed, and what remains conditional.

VERIFIED OFFLINE Document and trust contracts

DOCX structure checks, main-body extraction, source-bound evidence anchors, admission limits, fail-closed validation, workflow contracts, and automated verification.

DESIGNED · NOT DEPLOYED AWS operating architecture

Asynchronous intake, processing boundaries, bounded Bedrock use, metadata state, identity, encryption, observability, cost controls, deletion, and recovery.

CONDITIONAL · NOT IMPLEMENTED Enterprise retrieval and workflow

Permission-aware connectors, hybrid retrieval, controlled generation, document-native revisions, cross-portfolio comparison, approvals, and durable orchestration where justified.
