01 · The product

A document can remain familiar while its meaning changes.

Formatting diffs create noise. General chat produces answers without a durable review record. Textent keeps structure, evidence, requirements, and previous decisions connected as documents evolve.

ProblemImportant changes hide inside long documents.

New obligations, assumptions, exceptions, and decision impacts can be buried among harmless edits.

Product responseReduce the work to consequential findings.

Show what changed, why it matters, the paired evidence, and what needs human attention.

Durable resultKeep the decision with the source.

Accept, defer, reject, and later recheck without losing the original baseline or rationale.

01

Reuse

Prepare a trusted document for its next use.

Identify values to update, sections to protect, and content requiring review. Generate a controlled new copy without changing the original.

02

Compare

Find the revisions that change the decision.

Align related sections and distinguish altered meaning, obligations, assumptions, and risks from formatting or wording noise.

03

Decision Check

Check new work against a stable baseline.

Compare proposals and recommendations with requirements, reveal coverage gaps, and retain each reviewer’s disposition.

Baseline→Review→Human decision→Revised input→Bounded recheck

02 · From idea to system

The product work spans research, design, implementation, and verification.

I developed Textent as both a product and an architecture laboratory: shaping the problem, designing the experience, implementing the evidence path, and testing the decisions behind the system.

  1. 01Frame the problem

    Document reuse, version comparison, continuous requirements, and decision accountability.

  2. 02Design the experience

    Figma flows, review states, source evidence, and human dispositions.

  3. 03Build the workflow

    Structure-aware DOCX processing, FAISS retrieval, and LangGraph state.

  4. 04Control the AI

    Independent evidence validation, bounded execution, and human approval.

  5. 05Architect for AWS

    Identity, model access, storage, observability, cost limits, and shutdown controls.

  6. 06Challenge the result

    Regression tests, adversarial inputs, false-clear checks, and acceptance gates.

Why AI?

Language changes in ways that exact matching cannot understand.

AI helps relate differently worded requirements, identify changes in intent, and propose why a revision may matter.

Semantic layer

AI proposes meaning.

  • Relate differently written requirements
  • Identify intent, assumptions, and impact
  • Organize candidate evidence
Control layer

Software proves the finding.

  • Validate exact source anchors
  • Reject unsupported conclusions
  • Preserve workflow state and human authority
