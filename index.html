Decisions You Can Defend

Agentic document decision system · Reuse · Compare · Check

I created Textent to help teams reuse, compare, and continuously review large, versioned document sets without losing structure, evidence, requirements, or prior decisions. I designed its product direction, cloud-native architecture, and verified comparison path so every consequential change remains connected to its source and the human decision.

Why AI? Requirements often keep their intent while their wording, structure, and location change. AI helps relate meaning across versions and surface what deserves review; evidence checks and human approval keep the result bounded.
