CREATOR & PRODUCT ARCHITECT

Textent turns conflicting documents into decisions teams can defend.

Textent is designed for large, versioned document sets where structure, evidence, and prior decisions must survive every review. It reduces the work to consequential findings: what changed, why it matters, what needs attention, and what was ultimately decided.

Its three connected paths help teams safely reuse trusted documents, continuously compare revisions against a fixed baseline, and check proposals or recommendations against stated requirements.

I created the product direction, designed the AWS-native target architecture, and implemented a bounded comparison path using structure-aware DOCX processing, FAISS retrieval, LangGraph workflow state, independent evidence validation, and human decision gates for a cloud-native agentic AI product.

Why AI? Related requirements and decisions are often expressed differently across documents and revisions. AI helps connect that meaning while deterministic controls preserve the evidence and decision history.

See how the product and AI architecture work →
Relate meaning across documents Structure-aware parsing and section alignment connect differently worded requirements before judgment.
Challenge every finding FAISS retrieval brings paired evidence forward; deterministic validators reject unsupported or role-confused results.
Keep decision state human-owned LangGraph pauses the workflow for accept, defer, or reject and resumes without rewriting the source record.

Demonstrated through a controlled synthetic decision scenario.

PROOF BEHIND THE CASE STUDY

The architecture is backed by a repeatable evidence path, not a collection of technology labels.

Paired evidenceEvery finding stays linked to both source documents.
Failure checksUnsupported and role-confused findings are rejected.
Human decisionAccept, defer, or reject remains part of the record.
Controlled recheckA revision is evaluated without overwriting the prior result.

AWS reference architecture: CDK, CloudFront, Cognito, API Gateway, one safe-OFF Lambda, private S3, SSM controls, CloudWatch, budgets, and a scoped Amazon Bedrock boundary.

Enterprise extension: authorized OneDrive, SharePoint, Teams, and Slack sources; document-intelligence adapters; permission-aware retrieval; approvals; retention; and audit lineage.
